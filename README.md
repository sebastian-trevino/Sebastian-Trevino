## Featured Projects

### ⛽ [Gasoline Demand Forecasting — Sunoco](https://github.com/sebastian-trevino/sunoco-gasoline-demand-forecasting)
Cost-aware CatBoost ensemble that flags high-demand weeks three weeks ahead, the lead time Sunoco needs to move product.
- 87.5% recall on a fully held-out year (14 of 16 high-demand weeks caught), 63.6% precision, 0.82 ROC AUC
- 168 lagged features from 22 macro, energy-market, and mobility predictors across 744 weeks
- Selected the decision threshold by **minimizing expected cost**, not maximizing accuracy — a missed high-demand week was weighted 2x a false alarm, cutting false alarms 62% while holding recall

### 🏈 [Trinity Football Analytics — Expected Points Model](https://github.com/sebastian-trevino/Trinity-Football-Analytics)
End-to-end analytics workflow built from nothing for Trinity's football program, as the inaugural analytics intern.
- Generalized Additive Model (pyGAM) estimating expected points across **~16,000 plays and eight seasons**
- Derived EPA to evaluate play-level performance in situational context, surfacing the top eight high-impact variables
- Delivered through interactive Tableau and Plotly dashboards used by coaching staff
- Led a team of two interns and wrote the process documentation the program still runs on

### 📊 [NSSE Institutional Research — Senior Capstone](https://github.com/sebastian-trevino/trinity-nsse-student-engagement)
Longitudinal study of how experiential learning shapes student engagement, presented to Trinity's Institutional Research Office and the Dean of the School of Business.
- Merged multi-year survey data and engineered a binary experiential-learning variable
- Mixed-effects models on a 119-student cohort: experiential learners began with higher Academic Challenge scores (β = 5.42, p = .004) but converged with peers by senior year (β = −5.83, p = .003); Faculty Experience scores rose across all class years (β = 3.95, p = .039)
